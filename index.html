ABOUT THE ARTIST

Official Portrait

A Journey of Sound
and Soul

PRIME is more than an artist — he is a storyteller who paints vivid pictures through sound. Born from the streets and shaped by the rhythm of the city, his music carries the weight of real experiences and the beauty of raw emotion.

With a sound that fuses deep-groove textures, cinematic production, and soul-stirring lyrics, PRIME has carved out a unique space in the music world. Each track is a chapter — a testament to the blood, sweat, and tears poured into every note.

From rooftop sessions to global stages, PRIME continues to push boundaries, collaborating with visionary producers and artists who share his passion for authentic, groundbreaking music.
